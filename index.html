ABOUT

More than a group chat. Less than a company. Exactly what it needs to be.

Lings started as a small group of teenage programmers who liked building things together. Over time, it became a shared space for collaboration, experimentation, and learning through real work instead of empty theory.

We work on Linux-related projects, websites, utilities, system-level ideas, and weird experiments that feel too interesting not to pursue. Some projects are practical, some are exploratory, and some exist simply because we wanted to see if we could do them.

The point is not polish for its own sake. The point is momentum, curiosity, and making things that actually exist.

JOIN LINGS

We want people who like making real things.

You do not need to be perfect. You do need curiosity, consistency, and the ability to work with other people on shared projects.

Join the Discord server and talk to a contributor such as SfymmiK if you want to get involved.

English

Programming
