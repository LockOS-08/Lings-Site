About Lings

Lings is a small collective of teenage programmers who love building things
just for the fun of it. What started as a few friends experimenting with code
quickly turned into a collaborative project where we share ideas, learn from
each other, and create our own tools, websites, and software.

We believe programming is more than just a skill — it’s a way to explore ideas,
solve problems, and create something from nothing. At Lings, we experiment
with different technologies, build projects we find interesting, and push
ourselves to learn new things along the way.

Our projects range from web development and software tools to experimental
ideas that simply sounded fun to try. Not everything we make is perfect, but
everything we build helps us improve.

Lings is about learning, experimenting, and creating — together.
