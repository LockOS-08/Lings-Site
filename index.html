ABOUT

What Lings is

Lings started as a small group of teenage programmers who liked making things together. Over time, that became a shared space for building projects, exchanging ideas, improving skills, and learning through real work.

We work on websites, Linux-related projects, tools, experiments, and system-level ideas. Some projects are practical, some are exploratory, and some exist because they were simply too interesting not to try.

The goal is not to look polished for its own sake. The goal is to keep building, keep learning, and create things together.

CONTRIBUTING

How to join

English

At least B1 level for technical conversation and collaboration.

Age

Ideally 13 years old or older.

Programming

Above-basic knowledge of at least one programming language.

Teamwork

Ability to work with others and communicate in group projects.

Git

Know Git already, or be willing to learn it while contributing.

Activity

Stay somewhat active in discussions, development, or project work.

For further questions, join our Discord server and message a contributor such as SfymmiK.
