ABOUT

What Lings actually is

A shared space for building, learning, experimenting, and getting better through real projects.

Lings started as a small group of teenage programmers who liked making things together. Over time, that turned into a collaborative collective where we share ideas, explore technologies, and build software that teaches us something every time we touch it.

We work on websites, Linux-related projects, tools, experiments, and system-level ideas. Some projects are practical, some are chaotic, and some only exist because they sounded too interesting not to try.

We do not aim to be perfect. We aim to keep building, keep learning, and keep improving. That is what makes the whole thing worth it.

Lings is about curiosity, experimentation, and creation — together.

JOINING

Want to join Lings?

We are open to people who genuinely enjoy programming, learning, and working on real things together.

English

At least B1 level, enough for technical conversation and collaboration.

Age

Ideally 13 years old or older.

Programming

Above-basic knowledge of at least one programming language.

Teamwork

Ability to work with others and communicate in group projects.

Git

Know Git already, or be willing to learn it while contributing.

Activity

Stay somewhat active in discussions, development, or project work.

For further questions, join our Discord server and message a contributor such as SfymmiK.
